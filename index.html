03 / ARCHIVE NOTES

Know your source.

This is an unofficial community field guide for S.T.A.L.K.E.R. GAMMA 0.9.5. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by GSC Game World, the Anomaly team, or the GAMMA team.

Artifact statistics, identifiers, perk keys and crafting records are sourced from the Stalker GAMMA Item Database. Display names and perk descriptions are resolved from its English localization data when available. This guide uses an independent interface and visual presentation.

Resistance values and resistance caps are separate mechanics and are shown separately. The guide displays exported values as-is and does not calculate an overall artifact score.
